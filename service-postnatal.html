Postnatal Recovery & Wellness

Restore your core, rebuild strength, and regain confidence after delivery. A comprehensive recovery program for your physical and emotional well-being.

Reclaim Your Strength

The postpartum period requires special attention to help new mothers recover and regain their strength. Dr. Akanksha Raj's postnatal recovery program addresses the unique needs of women after delivery, focusing on physical recovery, core strengthening, and emotional wellness. Whether you had a natural birth or C-section, our personalized approach ensures a safe return to fitness.

Pelvic Floor

Rehabilitation to prevent incontinence and strengthen pelvic muscles.

Core Strength

Gradual progression to rebuild deep core strength and spinal stability.

Emotional Wellness

Support for emotional fitness and mental well-being during the transition to motherhood.

Recovery Timeline

Weeks 0-6 (Healing Phase)

Focus on rest, gentle breathing, and pelvic floor activation. Pain management for C-section scars.

Weeks 6-12 (Foundation)

Introduction of gentle core exercises, diastasis recti correction, and posture improvement.

Week 12+ (Strengthening)

Progressive strengthening, functional movements, and safe return to regular exercise or sports.

Frequently Asked Questions

When can I start exercising after delivery?

For normal delivery, you can start gentle pelvic floor exercises within days. For C-section, wait 6-8 weeks for more active exercise, but consult us early for safe recovery guidelines.

What is Diastasis Recti?

It is the separation of the abdominal muscles during pregnancy. If not treated, it can lead to a 'pooch' tummy and back pain. We provide specific exercises to close this gap.

Do you help with C-section recovery?

Yes, we specialize in scar tissue mobilization and safe exercises that protect your incision while rebuilding strength.
