Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No, you can book directly. However, if you have X-rays or MRI reports, please bring them along.

How long does recovery take?

It varies by condition. Acute injuries may take a few weeks, while chronic conditions or post-surgical rehab may take months. We provide a timeline after assessment.

Is the treatment painful?

Our goal is pain relief. Some manual techniques might cause slight discomfort, but we always work within your tolerance levels.
