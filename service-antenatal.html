Why Antenatal Physiotherapy?

Pregnancy is a beautiful journey that comes with its own set of physical challenges. Our antenatal care program is designed to support expectant mothers through every stage of pregnancy. Dr. Akanksha Raj, with her international certification in antenatal exercise, creates personalized programs that help manage pregnancy-related discomforts, prepare the body for delivery, and maintain optimal health for both mother and baby.

Pain Management

Effective relief for back pain, pelvic girdle pain, and sciatica common during pregnancy.

Active Pregnancy

Safe, trimester-specific exercise programs to maintain fitness and energy levels.

Labour Prep

Breathing techniques, pelvic floor exercises, and positioning for an easier delivery.

Posture Care

Guidance on body mechanics and posture to prevent strain as your baby grows.

Our Treatment Process

Initial Assessment

Frequently Asked Questions

Is physiotherapy safe during pregnancy?

Yes, when performed by a certified specialist like Dr. Akanksha, physiotherapy is safe and highly beneficial. We strictly follow safety guidelines for each trimester.

When should I start antenatal classes?

You can start as early as your first trimester after consulting your gynecologist. However, many women join in their second trimester when morning sickness subsides.

Can you help with back pain?

Absolutely. Back pain is very common during pregnancy. We use safe manual therapy and specific exercises to relieve pain and strengthen supporting muscles.
