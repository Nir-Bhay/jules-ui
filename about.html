Master's in Physiotherapy

A Legacy of Healing & Care

Dr. Akanksha Raj brings over 18 years of dedicated experience in physiotherapy, with a special focus on women's health and orthopedic care. Her journey in healthcare includes valuable experience at the South Central Railway Hospital, where she honed her skills in treating diverse patient populations.

Her international certification in antenatal and postnatal exercise has made her a trusted expert for expectant and new mothers in Bhopal. Dr. Akanksha believes in a holistic approach to healing, addressing not just physical symptoms but also the mental and emotional aspects of recovery.
